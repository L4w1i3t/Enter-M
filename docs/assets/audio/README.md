# Audio source and pending work

Checked September 25, 2026. `game/asset_definitions/def_audio.rpy` defines shared
aliases; `game/addons/Music Room/music_room.rpy` defines player-facing titles,
artists, catalog order, listen channels, and persistent compatibility keys.
Story `play`/`stop` statements establish actual placement. An alias or catalog
entry alone does not establish that a scene plays it.

The author's standing choices retain `enterm`, `quiz`, `safety`, and `holdmusic`.
Sunday's `day_b` and track-scene `daily` placements are authored decisions.
Changes to cue meaning require the author's direction. Phone message/call
sounds are owned by the Phone System and should not be duplicated in story
scripts merely because a scene mentions a phone.

[Music identity notes](../../keeping_tabs.txt) preserve supplied artist names
and unresolved credits. The Music Room is the implemented credit list; the
notes are not a second list of tracks waiting to be integrated.

The earlier ambience plan is still unimplemented planning unless an actual
playback call exists. Prioritize credible household/school/street/cafe/arcade
space and significant physical actions. Preserve silence and avoid diagnostic
stingers that explain a character's feelings. Audition suitable existing assets
before sourcing another recording; coordinate start/stop and route exits.

Music research and scene-by-scene suggestions were consolidated into
[audio history](../../history/audio.md). They are not current license proof or
listening results. Keep licenses/attribution beside the recordings they govern.
