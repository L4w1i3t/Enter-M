# Archive Panel CG Prompts

These are the production prompts used for the seven Archive hub panels. They
were generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 2026-08-27. Native
sources were normalized into 1280x720 CG Gallery masters, then downsampled to
384x216 Archive-hub panels. The hub displays them at 192x108.

## Shared direction

- Use case: `illustration-story`
- Asset type: landscape game UI Archive-panel mini-CG
- Medium: polished 2D anime visual-novel CG; crisp controlled linework, soft
  painterly cel shading, cohesive proportions, expressive but natural faces
- Lighting: warm practical or sunset key light with restrained cyan-teal edge
  light so the art belongs inside the Archive's near-black/cyan interface
- Framing: compose for a safe 16:9 crop; keep faces, hands, and the defining
  action in the central 80-85 percent; the image must read at 192x108
- Constraints: preserve the supplied identity anchors; use scene-specific poses;
  no baked UI frame; no readable text, letters, numbers, logos, signatures, or
  watermarks; no photorealism or chibi proportions
- Initial style reference: `game/addons/Sick Ass Anime OP/monikaclub.png`
- Set-consistency reference after the first panel: the selected Roster result

The built-in tool accepts at most five reference paths, so the six anchors were
assembled into a temporary 3x2 identity sheet. The sheet was identity/outfit
reference only; none of its sprite poses were to be copied.

> A spontaneous selfie-style group photo in the literature-club room near
> sunset, with exactly MC, Asahina, Matsuda, Monika, Sam, and Tsubaki. Matsuda is
> closest at lower-left, extending one white-gloved arm toward the camera and
> laughing. Tsubaki leans in beside her with an unexpectedly playful peace sign.
> MC is caught genuinely mid-laugh in the center. Monika smiles while straightening
> one crooked hoodie drawstring. Asahina pops in behind MC and makes bunny ears.
> Sam, the tallest, bends into the back of frame with a crooked grin. Give all six
> different gestures, head angles, postures, and expressions. Preserve relationship
> adjacency: Matsuda/Tsubaki, MC/Monika, Asahina/Sam. Preserve Matsuda's large
> high ponytail and her single narrow braid on her anatomical right. Do not reuse crossed arms,
> hand-on-hip, hand-behind-neck, clasped-waist, or neutral hanging-arm sprite poses.
> Exactly six people; no duplicates, omissions, fused bodies, or formal two-row
> school-photo staging.

The selected selfie render received one continuity edit: add Matsuda's missing
three-strand red braid on her anatomical right (viewer-left in this angle), tied
near the end with a small teal band, without changing the group composition.

## Runtime post-processing

Selected native source renders were normalized into the project's established
1280x720 CG format with a top-safe 16:9 crop and Lanczos downsampling:

```text
scale=1280:720:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase:flags=lanczos,
crop=1280:720:0:0,setsar=1
```

The hub panels are derived from those masters with:

```text
scale=384:216:flags=lanczos,setsar=1
```

Full-resolution files live in `game/assets/cg/archive_*.png` and are catalogued
under **Other > Archive Panels** in the CG Gallery. Hub panels live in
`game/assets/gui/archive_panels/` and are referenced from
`game/archive.rpy`. Neither set lives under `game/assets/archive/`, which is
excluded from release builds.
