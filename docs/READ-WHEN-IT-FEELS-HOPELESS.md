# Read this when it feels hopeless

This is for release week, and for any night after it. If you only read one
section, read the first one.

## The 60-second version

- **You're tired, not bad at this.** Feeling hopeless at 2 a.m. is almost
  always exhaustion talking. It sounds like the truth because it's loud.
- **Stop for tonight.** Drink water, eat something with a color, and go to
  sleep.
- **Tomorrow, do one checkbox,** the smallest one in the
  [plan](project/ch05_release_plan.md). A day that moves one box is a day that
  moved.
- **The game isn't going anywhere.** The door is already open.

## About the feedback, including mine

- **Your friends spent time in your world.** Feedback means they played it.
- **Their good news is the hard part.** "It works as slice of life" means
  people already like living there. You can't fake that, and you can't build
  it in a weekend. You already have it.
- **Their bad news is the fixable part.** Questions left open, fights that
  leave a mark, and cuts are all structure. None of it says you can't write.
  It says some of the furniture is in the wrong room.
- **The plan opened with this, and it's still true:** line for line, most of
  the prose is good. You asked for tough love, and that's what it was. It
  isn't a verdict on you.

## If Wednesday goes sideways

- **You already decided this was okay.** In your words: "if i don't get to it
  in time, then so be it." Hold yourself to that.
- **P0 alone is a release.** Everything past P0 makes it a better release, or
  becomes a patch. Almost every game you've played got patched after launch.
- **If it slips a day, it slips a day.** Matsuda would call it a dramatic
  entrance.
- **It's Chapter 0.5,** the half-step. It's allowed to be where the story
  starts, not where it peaks.

## Things that stay true on a bad day

- Chapter 0.5 is about 72,000 words, which is novel length.
- The whole game is 106,012 words across 9,292 dialogue boxes, with 97 menus
  and 371 screens (lint, October 9). Lint is clean, and it runs.
- You started with a DDLC mod and turned it into your own game.
- You built a cast people want to spend time with. Nobody can hand you that.
- Your theme is perseverance: life knocks you down, kicks you while you're
  down, and you get up, dust yourself off and keep going. You've said it comes
  from your own life, so you already know how this part goes. Shipping a
  chapter while you're tired isn't off-theme. It is the theme.

## One more thing

If the hopeless feeling ever stops being about the game, and it gets heavier
than a deadline, close the laptop and tell a real person. You matter more than
October 14. The game will wait for you. The door is already open.
