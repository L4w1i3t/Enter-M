GOALS FOR THE MAIN STORY:

By the end of Chapter 0.5:
	- Relationships should be established
	- World must be build and feel lived in
	- The core cast must be introduced
	- The day-by-day structure is cohesive without being boring (to the player)

By the end of June:
	- The core cast must have an established friend group
	- Day dedicated to Tsubaki's birthday

By the end of July:
	- Love triangle hints must start showing subtly
	- Matsuda/Monika rivalry

By the end of August:
	- At least 1 beach trip and at least 1 fireworks festival
	- At least 1 hang-out session at MC's house
	- At least 1 hang-out session at Matsuda's house
	- Day dedicated to Asahina's birthday

By the end of September:
	- Love triangle between Sam, Asahina, and Tsubaki in full bloom
	- The core cast goes to an anime convention
	- Day dedicated to Monika's birthday

By the end of October:
	- Halloween (without Asahina)
	- School Culture Festival
	- Asahina gets torn a new one by mom due to sleeping with Sam
	- Tsubaki is more abrasive
	- Fractures in the friend group due to love triangle and Matsuda/Monika rivalry
	- Matsuda's relationship with MC becomes more toxic and psychologically manipulative (subtly)
	- The "first domino" falls to kickstart the drastic tonal change (at the end of chapter 3)
		- It has to be truly shocking--high stakes to establish how the rest of the story is going to play out.
	- Mom finds out she has cancer
	- Day dedicated to Matsuda's birthday

By the end of November:
	- Sam's true nature and character must be completely unveiled at least to the main cast
	- Significantly less screentime for Tsubaki
	- Noticeable changes in Tsubaki, physically and personally
	- Mom discloses to MC that she has cancer
	- Day dedicated to Mom's birthday

By the end of December:
	- Tsubaki must be completely deteriorated a la Metamorphosis/Emergence
	- Tsubaki must be written out of the story without having an explicit death, moving away, etc (like the story forgets about her)
	- Matsuda must be completely dominant in the friendship with MC to a toxic level

By the end of January:
	- Kotonoha takes up a more major role in the final chapter
	- MC has distanced himself from the friend group as a whole
	- Much less screentime for Asahina, Matsuda, Sam, Monika
	- Tsubaki is completely erased from the story as if she never existed--not in the "must have been the wind" way, genuinely natural progression without any mention of her existence (I'm serious, this is part of the horror)
		- There should be no identifiable moment where Tsubaki is written out
	- Asahina must be conflicted about her relationship with Sam (like she sees the writing on the wall but can't let go emotionally)

By the end of February:
	- MC has finally found a suitable excuse to completely cut ties with Matsuda (cause it's easier to find a way out instead of cutting things off abruptly)
	- Monika has formally disbanded the literature club
	- Asahina and Sam are no longer in a relationship
	- Sam is effectively gone from the story

By March 7:
	- Graduation speeches (can be either MC, Monika, or Matsuda depending on how well the player did on exams throughout the game)
	- The player must genuinely miss the warm, casual feeling of Chapter 0.5
	- Most or all relationships must be either severed, fundamentally transformed, or irreparably damaged
	- The literature club should feel like a bygone era instead of something that was just recently disbanded
