A visual novel about one school year, told a day at a time.

Download the demo

Chapter 0.5 is free, for Windows, macOS, and Linux.

ENTER-M follows a graduating senior through his last school year, from the middle of May to the first week of March. He keeps his head low with his expectations, which his younger sister has opinions about. A friend from his club reads his poems with a pen in hand.

It follows a usual day-by-day routine, but how long exactly can that be maintained?

Snapshots

In the demo

Chapter 0.5 covers May 15 to May 31. When it ends, the Music Room opens, along with six side stories: Heads, Tails, Hina’s World, Purgatorium, The Girl with Every Face, and The Void Hunter. The arcade is open from the start.

Getting started

  1. Download the zip from the latest release.
  2. Unzip it wherever you like.
  3. Open ENTER-M.exe on Windows, ENTER-M.app on macOS, or ENTER-M.sh on Linux.

Some antivirus programs flag ENTER-M.exe. It’s a false positive: the launcher isn’t code-signed, which is common for independent games, and security tools treat new, unsigned programs with suspicion. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC,” choose “More info,” then “Run anyway.” If your antivirus quarantines a file, restore it and add the game folder as an exception. Only download the game from the releases page above, so you know the warning is about this build.
