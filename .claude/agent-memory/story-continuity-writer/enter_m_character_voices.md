**MC** (tag `mc`): see [[enter-m-voice-fingerprint]]. Numb, capable, cynical, emotionally avoidant, unreliable narrator. Sibling bite with Asahina is real but the narration must NOTICE when it crosses from teasing into cruelty (the harshest insults — "fatass," boiling-water threats, "be more popular if you talked less" — are flagged in critique as too far; soften or let MC register the wound).

**Monika** (tag `m`, `m_name`): warm, teasing, cute labels, affectionate deflection — but strongest when she WANTS something and has to risk asking (the library/Sunday invitation model). Let her be imperfectly wanting, tired, a little needy — not just perfectly supportive. Watch nickname overuse; labels should not replace intimacy. Speech: "~" trailing tildes, "haha," exclamation energy, occasional sharper word when comfortable ("holy hell," "bonehead," "Mr. Smartass"). As of Oct 2026, "shitass" in the live text is Asahina's word, not hers. Per-character crude registers: [[enter-m-crude-register]].

**Matsuda** (tag `ma`, `ma_name`): theatrical/ornate/archaic ("'Twas," "my good chap," "Hark," "comrade," radiant self-regard) — but ON PURPOSE and SELECTIVELY. Her ornate voice is armor; her PLAIN cracks are more revealing ("...Thank ya," "I overstepped," "I was hoping if I acted like it had been longer it would feel less embarrassing"). She can drop the bard when MC asks. She invents working titles ("Azure Eye"). Subtly meta-aware: occasional lukewarm reaction where intensity is expected; oddly precise reads under the clowning. Latches onto patterns/people. Don't make reading her exhausting even though she IS exhausting — give payoff per scene.

**Tsubaki** (tag `ts`, `ts_name`): insecurity is BEHAVIORAL — reveal through small actions (gripping the paper bag, delayed smiles, apologizing for taking up space, pressing a hand over her notebook). Avoid over-diagnosing her in dialogue. Apologizes too quickly, self-deprecates, likes hygiene/neat notes. Has quiet dry humor that's HERS ("Maybe the roof has phases too"). Translates Matsuda for others. Unrequited crush on Sam (hidden). Her routines must be specific now so later deterioration is readable.

**Asahina** (tag `ah`): MC's sister, FIFTEEN (docs/story/MAIN_STORY_PROSE_AND_INTENT_GUIDE.md; MC is seventeen). The guide's Sep 5 author demeanor table supersedes these June notes wherever they disagree. Defensive pride, wants recognition while pretending not to (poster arc). Uses teen online-speak as a verbal tic ("skill issue," "ngl"/"en-gee-el," "get rekt," "brotato chip") which MC narrates with cringe. Pattern: insult, denial, embarrassment, soft reveal — vary it. Food/eating beats are a loaded thread (do NOT let narration wave them away as "nothing" unless that's clearly MC's blind spot). Sets three places at the table by default even when complaining.

**Sam** (tag `sa`): Asahina's boyfriend "Sam" (Masayami Inugami). Plausible, charming, NEVER openly villainous. Danger is in deniability: concern that functions as surveillance, remembering things too precisely, kindness that subtly controls the room. Plant tiny wrong notes, not villain signals. Smooth delivery, turns awkwardness into shared jokes.

**Kotonoha** (tag `ko`): polite, exact, professionally flat, emotionally unreachable, faintly uncanny. Behavior over mystique ("Most people watch where cars were," tightening a loose glue cap, folding receipts exactly in half). Use cryptic lines ("You will") sparingly.

**Mom / Himeko** (tag `mom`): lawyer/counselor voice — practical register, breaks big problems into steps, dry ("Counselor mom, actually. Doctor mom bills more."). Warm but strict. "Trust" is her serious currency. Later cancer arc — keep her strain as ordinary adult fatigue/logistics (folders, late court calls), not ominous foreshadowing.

**Teachers/Coach:** Coach has bite without overperforming, sees through MC's self-deception, grounded. Class quiz scenes follow a menu pattern (right/wrong answer labels) and ideally tie thematically to that day's events.
